Original Bloodborne launcher sounds

The launcher extracts these effects locally from dvdroot_ps4/sound/sprj_main*.fsb:
hover.wav: CURSOL_MOVE (hover)
confirm.wav: menu_open (soft menu-opening confirmation)
change.wav: CURSOL_SELECT (selector, on/off switch and slider change)
start.wav: GAMESTART (main Play action)

Audio plays at 25% PCM amplitude. Cached WAVs retain their original samples;
Windows and game volume remain unchanged. The speaker button in the lower-right
corner toggles launcher sound and remembers the setting across launches.
Window caption buttons and the speaker button are silent.

Valid custom clips are preserved. The old select.wav is left untouched but is
no longer played; confirm.wav is extracted automatically during the next launch.
Missing banks leave optional audio silent without blocking the launcher.
No original game audio is redistributed. Decoding runs in an isolated worker;
a complete cache skips extraction on subsequent starts.
